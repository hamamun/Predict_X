PREDICT-X User Guide

Version 2.00 — 6-layer scoring, the Aladin memory bank, optional auto-trading, and staircase trade protection.

1. What PREDICT-X Does

PREDICT-X is a MetaTrader 5 Expert Advisor. It analyses closed candles, scores market conditions across six layers, predicts BUY / SELL / NO TRADE, and — only if you switch it on — places and manages trades for you.

Version 2.00 adds the Aladin memory bank: the EA rehearses its own scoring over the last 3,000 bars of your chart, remembers how similar setups actually ended, and uses that history to place smarter stops, resize or refuse weak setups, and upgrade entries it has high confidence in.

Native MT5 / MQL5 only. No martingale. No grid. Closed-bar signals only. Single position per symbol. No external APIs or DLLs.

3. Installation

Copy the files to these exact paths inside your MT5 data folder (File → Open Data Folder):

MQL5/Experts/PREDICT-X/PREDICT-X.mq5
MQL5/Experts/PREDICT-X/Include/*.mqh
MQL5/Indicators/PREDICT-X/Indicators/SuperTrend.mq5
MQL5/Indicators/PREDICT-X/Indicators/TTMSqueeze.mq5
MQL5/Indicators/PREDICT-X/Indicators/KeltnerChannel.mq5
  1. Open MetaEditor and compile the three indicators first.
  2. Compile PREDICT-X.mq5 (it pulls in the 18 include files automatically).
  3. In MT5, refresh the Navigator and attach the EA to an M5, M15 or M30 chart.
  4. Enable Algo Trading in MT5 if you want the EA to place orders.

Supported timeframes are M5, M15 and M30. Any other timeframe still loads but falls back to the M15 preset and the panel shows an unsupported TF warning.

4. First Run — What to Expect

The first time you attach the EA to a symbol and timeframe, Aladin has no memory yet. It builds one:

  1. The EA rehearses its own scoring over the last 3,000 closed bars of that chart, in chunks of 200 bars per pass so the terminal never freezes.
  2. While rehearsing, the panel shows Aladin learning — building history (x% / N setups).
  3. From then on, every live signal and its real outcome is appended to the same memory file, so the bank keeps growing.

Until 30 resolved outcomes exist, Aladin is show-only: it displays its read of the market but changes nothing. After that, Phase B actions arm automatically.

Rehearsal is per symbol + timeframe. Attaching to a new chart builds a separate memory file for it.

6. Scoring and Signal Tiers

Every closed bar is scored by six layers plus a candle bonus, capped at 100:

  • Layer 1 — Smart Money / SMC: order blocks and structure.
  • Layer 2 — Trend and momentum: SuperTrend, ADX, momentum continuation.
  • Layer 3 — Value: VWAP, Keltner, session, spread.
  • Layer 4 — Higher-timeframe confluence.
  • Layer 5 — Markov chain state transitions.
  • Layer 6 — Online AI confirmation.
  • Candlestick confirmation bonus (max +5).
ScoreTierTypical entry
85 – 100VERY STRONGMarket
70 – 84STRONGMarket if momentum is healthy; limit if extended
55 – 69MEDIUMVWAP / order-block limit
below 55WEAK / NO TRADENo trade

The minimum score is 55 by default, and 60 on M5. A score below the minimum never becomes a trade, no matter what the memory bank says.

7. Aladin Memory Bank new

How it works

  1. Rehearsal. On first run the EA replays its own scoring over the last 3,000 closed bars and records how each simulated setup would have ended (SL, TP1, TP2, breakeven, or timeout), including the worst drawdown before the result.
  2. Live logging. Every real signal, its planned entry / SL / TP1 / TP2, and its actual outcome are appended to the same bank, forever.
  3. Lookup. On each new signal the EA finds the 50 most similar past setups (same 12-feature profile the Online AI uses, same direction) and reports their win rate, TP1 rate, and typical dip in ATR.

Memory lives in one plain CSV per symbol and timeframe:

MQL5/Files/PREDICTX-MEM_<SYMBOL>_<TIMEFRAME>.csv
e.g. MQL5/Files/PREDICTX-MEM_XAUUSD_M15.csv

Deleting this file simply makes Aladin rehearse again from scratch. Nothing else in the EA is affected.

The FUTURE VIEW panel block

Shows the verdict for the current bar, for example:

Aladin active — similar 50 | win 68% | TP1 81% | dip 0.6 ATR.
History OK — no change needed.

Followed by numbered step lines covering memory, lookup, refuse, SL/TP, resize and entry. Status words:

8. Entry Logic

  • VERY STRONG → market entry.
  • STRONG with healthy momentum and not extended → market entry.
  • STRONG but extended → market only if ADX is very strong and spread is low; otherwise a limit.
  • MEDIUM near the upper end with strong momentum → market.
  • MEDIUM normally → VWAP or order-block limit.

The pending setup is refreshed on every closed bar; stale or invalid orders are updated or cancelled. A pending signal is also cancelled instantly if SuperTrend flips against it.

With Aladin active, a GO verdict can promote a limit to market and extend the pending expiry by two bars.

9. Broker-Aware TP / SL

Levels are adjusted to satisfy your broker's stop and freeze distance rules:

  • BUY TP must sit above the minimum broker distance; SELL TP below it.
  • BUY SL must sit below it, SELL SL above it — when an initial SL is used.
  • If the adjustment wrecks the reward-to-risk ratio, the setup is rejected outright.

10. Trade Management

Before TP1 — early profit locks

  • 1/3 of the way to TP1 → stop moves to breakeven plus a spread buffer.
  • 2/3 of the way → stop locks in the 1/3 profit level.
  • 90% of the way → stop locks in the 2/3 profit level.

The EA tracks the best favourable price reached before TP1, so a fast spike through a level is never missed after a pullback. The stop never moves backwards.

At TP1

  • Closes 50% of the position, if the broker's minimum volume allows a partial close.
  • Moves the stop to breakeven on the remainder.

After TP1 — running the remainder

  • Once price travels halfway from TP1 to TP2, the stop locks to TP1. TP1 becomes the floor.
  • A giveback trail then protects the remaining profit.
  • SuperTrend, ATR and profit-lock trailing continue to manage the runner.

11. Staircase Protection new

This is the layer that banks profit on the way to TP1 instead of waiting for the stop to be hit.

  1. Once floating profit reaches 1.0 × the trade's risk (Protect Trigger), the staircase arms.
  2. The distance from entry to TP1 is split into buckets: entry → 1/3 → 2/3 → 90% → TP1.
  3. Each time price enters a higher bucket, the stop is raised to that bucket's floor.
  4. Inside a bucket, the position closes early if price either touches the bucket floor or gives back 30% (Protect Giveback) of the bucket's favourable move from its peak.

Safety: this layer never closes a losing trade. If floating P/L is not positive, it does nothing and leaves the exit to the normal stop or early-lock logic.

The bucket floors are the same 1/3, 2/3 and 90% levels used by the early profit locks, so the two systems agree rather than fight each other. After TP1 the separate post-TP1 trail takes over.

12. Active Prediction Monitor

After entry, the EA keeps re-checking the prediction on every closed bar:

  • Moderate weakness → tightens the stop only.
  • No-trade / heavy weakness / opposite signal → closes, but only if profit is above the broker-cost buffer.
  • Never closes a losing trade just because the prediction changed.

13. Online AI

  • Fewer than 20 evaluated signals → learning mode, bonus +0. The panel counts up (ONLINE AI LEARNING 7/20).
  • After 20 outcomes, if the AI's direction agrees with the signal it adds +5 at 55–70% confidence, or +10 above 70%.
  • Below 55% confidence it contributes nothing — the panel shows CAUTION or NEUTRAL. Nothing is ever deducted.
  • It never subtracts points and never trades alone.
  • The model is per chart symbol and is persisted in MT5 Global Variables.

This is a separate, simpler model from the Aladin memory bank. Both can be on at the same time — Aladin is turned off with Enable Aladin, the AI with Enable Online AI Scoring Enhancement.

14. Candlestick Confirmation

A small support layer only:

  • BUY: bullish engulfing, hammer / pin bar, morning star, strong bullish close.
  • SELL: bearish engulfing, shooting star / pin bar, evening star, strong bearish close.
  • Being near VWAP or an order block can add a small bonus.
  • Maximum bonus +5. It never subtracts and never creates a trade by itself.
  • An opposite-candle warning can block a market entry.

15. The Panels

Main (left) panel

Standard top-to-bottom layout: regime, total score, SIGNAL with a plain BUY / SELL / HOLD direction, the six scoring layers, market context, signal state, the ALADIN / FUTURE VIEW block with its step lines, a plain-language summary, and the last action taken with its time.

The real score stays visible even while a gate (spread, session, regime, or Aladin) is blocking the trade, so you can always see what the engine actually thinks.

Order manager (right panel)

Appears when a setup, order or trade exists. Shows entry, SL, TP1, TP2, breakeven, lot, reward-to-risk, live P/L, early lock state, post-TP1 state, trailing state, and the last action.

Both panels are removed cleanly when you detach the EA. Chart arrows and projection lines are drawn only for valid, tradeable signals.

17. Safety Notes

  • If Use Initial Stop Loss = false, there is no broker-side stop until the protection logic moves it. That is the default.
  • Auto Trading is off by default. The EA will not place orders until you switch it on.
  • Test on a demo account first.
  • Manual orders are not managed unless they carry the PREDICT-X magic number 26070501.
  • A trade opened on one timeframe cannot be signalled closed by another timeframe.
  • Aladin actions stay inside your risk plan: refuse and resize only ever reduce exposure, never increase it.
  • If the memory file cannot be read, Aladin disables itself and the EA continues on the classic path. It will not guess.
